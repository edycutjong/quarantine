title: "Tax charged on the coupon amount"
---
A 100.00 order with a 20.00 coupon and 10% tax totals 90.00. Tax should be on
what the customer actually pays (80.00), so the total should be 88.00.

Repro: `invoice_total(Decimal("100.00"), Decimal("20.00"), Decimal("10"))`
returns 90.00.
